Common questions

Can I build an ADU on my property?

Most likely. California law allows an ADU on most lots that have a single-family home, and many lots can add a junior ADU as well. Your city or county still sets details like height, setbacks and utility hookups, so we check those for your exact address during the free consultation.

How much does an ADU cost in the Central Valley?

It depends mostly on size, site work (how far utilities have to run, grading, access) and finishes. Garage conversions usually cost the least; a detached two-bedroom costs the most. After the consultation we give you a written estimate for your lot, not a range off a website.

How long does it take?

It varies by city and plan. Permits can take anywhere from several weeks to a few months, and construction usually takes several months after that. We'll give you a timeline for your property up front.

Can I put housing for farm workers on my land?

Often, yes. Agricultural parcels can usually add worker housing, but the rules depend on your county's zoning and California's employee housing requirements. Tell us where the property is and we'll find out what it allows.

Do you handle the plans and permits?

Yes. We prepare the plans, submit them to your city or county, answer their comments and build the unit, so you have one team from the first visit to move-in.
